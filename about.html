About

I help startups move toward action.

I'm a CTO. My job is to move startups from talking about the thing to shipping it — turning an idea, a deck, or a stalled roadmap into something real that people can open, use, and pay for.

Early-stage companies rarely stall for lack of ideas. They stall on decisions: what to build first, what to cut, which stack, which hire, what done actually means. I make those calls and own the outcome — architecture and stack selection, infrastructure and CI/CD release pipelines, and recruiting and leading cross-functional teams across mobile, backend, and design.

Over the last nine years I've worked across the full stack and the full company lifecycle — freelancer, lead developer, and CTO four times over. Flutter, Dart, Firebase, and AWS are the tools I reach for most, but the tools are the easy part. The work is getting a team from zero to a production-grade MVP fast, then putting the engineering standards, code review, and release process in place so it survives what comes after.
